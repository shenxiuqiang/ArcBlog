| `package.json` 依赖 | `@ocap/client` `^1.30.24`、`@ocap/wallet` `^1.30.24`、`@arcblock/did-ext` `^1.30.24`、`@arcblock/did-util` `1.30.24`、`bip39` `^3.1.0` | `node -e "const d=require('./package.json');console.log(d.dependencies)"` |
