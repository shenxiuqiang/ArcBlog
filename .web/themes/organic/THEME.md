# organic

Eco-friendly natural aesthetic with forest green -- warm, earthy design inspired by nature.

## Design DNA

- **Typography**: Lora serif headings (italic available) + Raleway body at weights 300-600, a warm literary pairing
- **Signature**: Forest green #228B22 primary + gold accent on warm cream #FAF7F2, with soft green-tinted borders #c5d5b5
- **Effects**: Frosted glass header (rgba 90% + blur 12px), subtle radial green gradient overlays (6% opacity), organic dot patterns, leaf-green hover borders
- **Cards**: Warm cream background with 1px green-tinted borders, 16px border-radius (the most rounded of all themes), soft shadow on hover

## Component Overrides

4 component overlays that bring natural warmth to the base components.

- `portal-header`: Frosted glass header (rgba cream 90% + blur 12px), green-tinted border, subtle green shadow on scroll, 1120px container
- `hero-banner`: Warm surface #f0ede5 background with radial green gradient overlay (6% opacity), CSS-only organic dot pattern, centered serif title, 7rem top padding
- `content-card`: 3-column grid with 2rem gaps, cream background cards with green-tinted 1px borders, 16px radius, green border and lift on hover, 1120px container
- `product-showcase`: 3-column grid with 2rem gaps, matching 16px radius cards with green-tinted borders, smooth 0.3s hover transitions with shadow and border-color change
