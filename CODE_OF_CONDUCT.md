# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds the project: being kind and specific in review comments; assuming the
other person had a reason and asking what it was; giving and accepting technical criticism about the
code, not the person; and saying "I could not verify this" instead of guessing.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report abuse through GitHub: open a **private** report via
<https://github.com/shenxiuqiang/ArcBlog/security/advisories/new> (it accepts conduct reports too and
keeps them off the public timeline), or contact the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.
Maintainers will review and may warn, remove content, or block accounts. Maintainers who do not follow
this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
